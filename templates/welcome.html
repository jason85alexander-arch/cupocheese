📲 Put the COC on your home screen

Get the 🧀 icon on your phone so the COC opens like a real app, full screen with no browser bars.

iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Open cupocheese.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android (Chrome):

  1. Open cupocheese.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app if it pops up, or tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen), then confirm.

Don't see the option? Refresh the page and try again. Your microphone works in the app the same as in the browser.
